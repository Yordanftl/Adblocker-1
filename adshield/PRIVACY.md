# Privacy Policy

AdShield collects **nothing**.

- All filtering happens locally, inside your browser's
  declarativeNetRequest engine.
- The extension makes **no network requests** of its own — there is no
  telemetry, no analytics, no update phone-home for filter lists.
- Settings (toggle state, whitelist, custom domains, blocked counters) are
  stored only in `chrome.storage.local` on your device and are never
  transmitted anywhere.
- The extension does not read, log, or modify page content beyond hiding
  elements that match its cosmetic filter selectors.

If you publish a fork, you are responsible for keeping it just as private.
